Live Formula Preview Mathematical Precision
EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)n] / [(1 + r)n − 1]
  • P (Principal): Vehicle On-Road Price − Down Payment
  • r (Monthly Rate): Annual Rate % ÷ 12 ÷ 100
  • n (Months): Loan Tenure in Years × 12
  • Zero Interest Case: Handled gracefully as P ÷ n with no division errors.
