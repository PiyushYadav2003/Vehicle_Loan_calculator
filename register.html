Demonstration Terms of Service
1. Academic Purpose

This web application is developed solely for college Web Technology demonstration, laboratory assignments, and viva evaluation.

2. Mathematical Estimations

Calculations performed by the Vehicle Loan EMI Calculator are mathematical estimates based on reducing balance formulas. Actual bank loans may incorporate processing fees, documentation charges, pre-EMI interest, and local taxes.

3. Client-Side Data Storage

All user information, passwords, and calculation histories are stored on your local computer's browser storage. No data is transmitted to an external web server or third-party cloud.
